Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about The Credence Group.

The Credence Group is a diversified holding company with seven subsidiaries operating across real estate, finance, consulting, and media industries — all united by a commitment to integrity and excellence.

Our subsidiaries operate under an 'Integrated Verticality' model. Capital flows into real estate, which is built by our construction arm, financed by our lending division, and marketed by our media company — creating a complete value chain.

The Credence Group is headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

For private equity and investment inquiries, please contact us at inquiries@credencegroup.com and our capital team will be in touch.
